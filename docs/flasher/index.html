open-airzone-gateway — web flasher

Plug the board into this computer with USB (Chrome or Edge on a desktop), then click the button. Unplug the board from the Airzone bus first: never power it from USB and from the bus terminal at the same time.

After flashing, the board opens the access point airzone-gw-XXXX; connect to it with a phone to enter your Wi-Fi and MQTT settings. It starts in listen mode and never transmits on the bus until you switch it to master mode.

Unofficial project, not affiliated with Airzone.
