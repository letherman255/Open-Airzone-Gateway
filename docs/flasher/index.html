Open Airzone Gateway

Put open firmware on a blank Waveshare ESP32-S3-RS485-CAN, right from this page.

What you need

  • A Waveshare ESP32-S3-RS485-CAN board
  • A USB-C data cable (some charge-only cables will not work)
  • A computer with Chrome or Edge (Web Serial is not available on phones or Safari)

Before you plug it in

Unplug the board from the Airzone bus first. Never power it from USB and from the bus terminal at the same time: this can damage the board.

This browser cannot flash over USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing only works on a secure (HTTPS) page. Open the official page at https://letherman255.github.io/Open-Airzone-Gateway/.

What happens next

  1. Join the open Wi-Fi network airzone-gw-XXXX that the board creates.
  2. Enter your Wi-Fi and your MQTT broker details.
  3. Open http://<gateway IP> and set the admin password.

The gateway starts in listen mode and never transmits on the bus until you switch it to master mode.
